# Phase 2: engage

**Status:** not started. Needs Phase 1 and Meta permissions for comments and DMs.
**Goal:** the daily habit: every comment and DM in one place, answered fast, in the creator's voice.

## What ships

- [ ] **One inbox:** Instagram and YouTube comments and DMs in one list, filters, assign to a teammate.
- [ ] **Keyword auto-replies** (from AutoDM) and **DM flows** (multi-step, within Meta's messaging rules).
- [ ] **Echo:** sorting, drafted replies in your voice, escalation of angry/sensitive/brand messages.
- [ ] **People:** fans, leads, brands, with history; brand messages hand off to Money.
