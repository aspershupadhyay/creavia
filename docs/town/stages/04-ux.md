## The direction (2026-11-26)

The owner asked for Creavia's UX to be handled the way **Town** (town.com, an AI work assistant) handles its own:
the assistant is the front door, it visibly learns how you work, it reports work as finished outcomes that wait for
your OK, and repeat work becomes a library of routines. We take those **patterns**, adapted to creators; we never
copy Town's name, characters, art, words or layout (Creavia has Vee and the crew, its own look and its own copy).
The first draft of flows and wireframes is the UX blueprint page (an artifact, linked from the plan doc); what follows
is the plan it commits to.

### Ten patterns we build on

| # | Pattern (as Town does it) | In Creavia | Where it lands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The ask box is the front door:** a large "Ask … anything" field on the first screen, with real examples | Home opens with **Ask Vee**, prefilled with rotating real asks from this creator's work ("Turn yesterday's Reel into a Short"); the same box is ⌘K and the Vee panel | Phase 0: the frame, Home |
| 2 | **Each person gets their own assistant**, met by name at sign-up | **Meet Vee** is the first onboarding step; on a team, each person's Vee knows their own work (one crew per person, not one bot for the workspace) | Phase 0: Accounts; Phase 1: memory |
| 3 | **"Helpful from hello": it learns you before you teach it** | After connecting Instagram/YouTube, Vee reads the last 30 posts and shows a first **creator profile** in about a minute: niche, voice, best formats, best times, audience. You correct it; you never fill a questionnaire | Phase 0: onboarding; Phase 1: memory |
| 4 | **Memory you can see: a Wiki** (Overview, Recent updates, Goals, Projects, Profile, People) | **Your page** (working name): Overview, Voice and style, Audience, Goals, Projects, People (collaborators, brands), What Vee learned this week. Every fact links to where it came from and can be edited or deleted | Phase 1: Ask Vee memory |
| 5 | **Work reported in the first person, as outcomes, waiting for OK:** "I've drafted a reply offering two open times… once you approve" | Every crew result is a card in that voice with the thing itself attached and one button: "I drafted 6 replies to comments on *Mic test*; 2 look like leads. **Review 6 replies**." No chat bubbles for results | Phase 0: Tasks foundation; every house |
| 6 | **Asks first, by default; you decide what it may do alone** | Ask-first on anything that posts, sends, spends or deletes (agents.md); per routine, an **autonomy setting**: Suggest · Prepare · Do then tell | Phase 1: agent runtime |
| 7 | **Routines: a library of ready-made automations,** filtered by role, plus **"Describe your own"** in one sentence and **Build it** | Recipes become **Routines** (same idea, plainer word): a library by creator type (Reels/Shorts, YouTube, podcast, with an editor). Featured: Morning brief, 24-hour post check, Comment → DM, Weekly content plan, Brand-deal spotter. "Every Friday at 4 pm, plan next week's posts from my best ideas" → Build it | Phase 1 (library), Phase 2 (Inbox routines) |
| 8 | **The briefing is the daily habit:** a morning briefing (email, app, audio) | **Your morning brief:** what posts today, what's waiting, how yesterday's post did, one thing to make next; in the app, by email and as a 1-minute audio on the phone. Weekly on the free plan, daily on paid | Phase 1 |
| 9 | **Integrations shown as work done, not logos:** each card is a sentence of what it did in that tool | Settings → Connectors: each connector's card says what Vee does with it ("Instagram: I reply to comments with your saved answers and DM the link") and its status light | Phase 0/1: connectors |
| 10 | **Trust in plain words, everywhere it matters:** asks first, no training on your data, encrypted | A short trust strip in onboarding, Settings and the connect dialogs: Vee asks before anything goes out; your content never trains a model; what each connector can see | Phase 0: onboarding; Stage 9 |

### The first ten minutes (target flow)

1. **Sign up** (email or Google) → name the workspace.
2. **Meet Vee:** one screen, Vee says what it will do and what it never does without asking.
3. **Connect** Instagram or YouTube (skippable, saying what you lose).
4. **Helpful from hello:** Vee shows your creator profile from your last 30 posts; you fix anything wrong.
5. **First win:** Vee offers three things it can do now (schedule the post you're working on, draft replies to waiting
   comments, start a weekly plan); one tap each, each ending in an outcome card.
6. **Your first routine:** "Want a morning brief?" → on.

### What stays Creavia's own

- **The look:** matte black, marigold as the one accent, Geist (docs/DESIGN.md), with Vee and the crew in their own
  hand-made illustrated style. Town's warmth comes from hand-drawn characters and an editorial serif on paper; our
  equivalent is the crew's illustrations and an editorial display face kept for the big moments (Home's greeting,
  onboarding, empty states) while working screens stay calm.
- **The houses and objects** (town-map.md): Town is one assistant across your tools; Creavia is a whole studio, so the
  ask box and outcome cards sit inside the houses rather than replacing them.
