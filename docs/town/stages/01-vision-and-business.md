# Stage 1: vision and business

**Status:** drafted (this file). **Done when:** the owner can explain in one minute who it is for, why they switch, and
how it makes money.

- **Problem:** creators pay for 8–12 tools that do not talk to each other, and spend hours a week moving files and
  copying numbers between them.
- **Vision:** the one place a creator runs their whole business, with a crew of agents doing the busywork.
- **Positioning:** Creavia is the creator's studio and team in one app: make, post, answer, measure and get paid, with
  agents that do the work for you.

## Competitors (each owns one house)

| House | Examples | How Creavia wins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create | ElevenLabs, Descript, CapCut, Canva | Free on your own computer; connected to posting and results |
| Calendar | Later, Buffer, Metricool, Hootsuite | Tempo fills and posts; tied to content and results |
| Inbox | ManyChat | Every platform, your voice, leads flow into Money |
| Analytics | vidIQ, TubeBuddy | Insights become ideas and drafts in one click |
| Money | Beacons, Stan, Linktree, Passionfroot, Wave | Deals, content, reports and invoices in one thread |
| Ideas | Notion | Built for creators, fed by your own results |

## Business model

Freemium (every feature free with local models and own keys; paid for cloud AI, accounts, automations, teams). Later:
a marketplace cut on paid plugins and recipes, agency plans.

## Unit economics to work out (Stage 6 metering gives real numbers)

Cost of a free user per month · cost of a paid user per month (GPU, AI, storage) · free→paid conversion · monthly
churn · cost to win a creator.

## Moat

1. Memory: after months the crew knows the creator. 2. Connected data: content ↔ results ↔ money exists only here.
3. Free that is truly free (local models). 4. Open to every AI: the place Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini act for a creator.
