# Stage 10: beta

**Status:** not started. **Done when:** at least 40% of beta creators are active weekly after 8 weeks, and most have
cancelled at least one tool.

1. **Alpha** (owner and a few friends, 2–4 weeks): daily use, same-week fixes, the owner's own content made in Creavia.
2. **Closed beta** (~50 creators, 6–8 weeks): from research and the owner's network; a private channel; a weekly call;
   every piece of feedback logged and answered.
3. **Open beta** (waitlist, in waves): free plan for all; paid plans tested at real prices.

| Measure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Activation | New creators who schedule a first post in their first session |
| Weekly active creators | Post through Creavia every week |
| Tools cancelled | Subscriptions each creator dropped |
| Agent trust | Drafts accepted unchanged; dials turned up over time |
| Retention | Still active after 4 and 8 weeks |
| Willingness to pay | Who picks a paid plan at the tested price |
