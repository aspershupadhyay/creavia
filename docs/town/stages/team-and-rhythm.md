# Team and rhythm

A small team with AI can run every stage if each role has an owner, even when one person holds several.

| Role | Owns | At the start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | Vision, research, PRDs, priorities | The owner |
| Design | UX, design system, screens | The owner + a freelance product designer; Claude for drafts |
| Engineering | Architecture, build, quality, releases | Claude doing most code, a person reviewing; an engineer when revenue allows |
| AI and agents | Agent behaviour, evals, costs | Claude, the owner approving |
| Growth | Website, content, partners, community | The owner and partner creators |
| Support and trust | Help centre, replies, policies, reviews | The owner, Vee answering first |

## Weekly rhythm
- Monday: pick the week's goals from the roadmap.
- Every day: build, test, ship to staging.
- Thursday: demo; test with one or two creators.
- Friday: release, update docs/town and the doc, record feedback, check metrics.
