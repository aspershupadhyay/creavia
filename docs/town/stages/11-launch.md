# Stage 11: launch and go-to-market

**Status:** not started. **Done when:** launch-week sign-ups meet the target, with no outage and support answering
within a day.

## Before launch
- Website: what it is, a 60-second demo, the town, pricing, download, the guide, policies.
- Pricing page, free plan first, with a "what you can cancel" calculator.
- Proof: 10 beta creators' stories with real numbers (hours saved, tools cancelled, results).
- Docs, the in-app guide for every house, a help centre.

## Channels
| Channel | How |
| --- | --- |
| Creators as partners | Beta creators get a free paid plan and a referral share |
| Content | Reels, Shorts, tutorials of Creavia doing real work, made with Creavia (videos are made in aspershupadhyay/content-creation) |
| Communities | Creator Discords, Reddit and X threads comparing tools |
| Launch platforms | Product Hunt and similar, on a planned day |
| Referrals | Free months for each creator who joins and posts |
| Templates and recipes | Shareable recipes that bring creators in |

## Launch day
1. Waitlist opens in waves. 2. Partners post together; demo and stories go live. 3. Dashboards, errors and support
watched all day.
