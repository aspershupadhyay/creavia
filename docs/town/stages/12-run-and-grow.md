# Stage 12: run and grow

**Status:** after launch. **Done when:** never; this stage is the business.

## Running it
- Monitoring and alerts: API, posting, DMs, agents, payments, GPU pool; a public status page.
- Targets: 99.9% uptime for the core; scheduled posts out within 2 minutes of their time.
- Incidents: runbook, fix, a written note of what happened and what changes.
- Backups daily; a restore drill monthly.
- Support: help centre, in-app chat with Vee first and a person behind it; replies within a working day.
- Releases weekly on the web; app updates through the in-app updater.
- Costs watched per workspace and feature against plan prices.

## Growing the business
| Metric | Watch for |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly active creators (north star) | Steady weekly growth |
| Activation | First scheduled post in the first session |
| Free to paid | Upgrades within 60 days |
| Paid churn | Monthly cancellations |
| Net revenue retention | Upgrades and seats outgrow cancellations |
| Tools replaced | Subscriptions cancelled per creator |
| Cost per active creator | Falling as usage grows |

Every quarter: talk to creators, update the town map, re-score the roadmap, choose the next houses and connectors.
