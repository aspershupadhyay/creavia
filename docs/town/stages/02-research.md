# Stage 2: research

**Status:** not started. **Done when:** the top ten pains are written with real quotes, and the first focus
(short-form creators) is confirmed or changed by the evidence.

1. Recruit 15 creators: 8 short-form, 4 long-form or podcast, 3 with an editor, manager or agency.
2. 45-minute interviews: walk through last week, every tool opened, what they paid, where they copied and pasted,
   what they hate, what they would pay to never do again.
3. Collect their tool bills (subscriptions and monthly spend).
4. Shadow two creators for a working day.
5. Write it up in `docs/town/research/`:

| Deliverable | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Personas (3–4) | Goals, tools, spend, frustrations, a quote |
| Jobs to be done | "When I…, I want to…, so I can…", ranked by pain and frequency |
| Journey map | Idea to paid: tool, time, pain, where Creavia removes it |
| Opportunity list | Each pain scored by how many creators have it and how much it hurts |
